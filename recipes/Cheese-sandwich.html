Cheese Sandwich

Description

A quick, comforting sandwich with crispy golden bread and melted cheese inside. Perfect for a fast lunch or a late-night snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter

Steps

  1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place one slice butter-side down in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the cheese slices on top.
  4. Place the second bread slice on top, butter-side up.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes until golden, then flip.
  6. Cook another 2-3 minutes until the other side is golden and cheese is melted.
