Chicken Pasta

Description

A quick, creamy pasta dish with tender chicken, garlic, and Parmesan cheese.

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 250 g chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 200 ml cooking cream

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for about 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the cream and add Parmesan cheese. Stir until the sauce becomes creamy.
