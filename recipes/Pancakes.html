Pancakes

Description

this taste pretty good and i like it

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1.5 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Whisk in eggs and milk until smooth.
  3. Pour batter onto a hot, greased pan.
  4. Flip once bubbles form on the surface.
  5. Serve warm with syrup.
